# Credits

Third-party fonts and libraries used by the home page.

## Design

The home page follows ForgeQE's own explainer video (`/media/main.mp4`): its dark grid, its two-line headings, its
dotted connectors and its 3D pixel icons, redrawn as SVG in `/img/icons` (`website/icons`). The pictures are ForgeQE's
own recordings (`/media`).

## Fonts (Ubuntu Font Licence 1.0)

- Ubuntu and Ubuntu Mono — https://design.ubuntu.com/font/

Self-hosted in `/fonts` as `ubuntu-latin-*.woff2`, `ubuntu-latin-ext-*.woff2` and `ubuntu-mono-latin-*.woff2`
(@fontsource/ubuntu 5.1.0 and @fontsource/ubuntu-mono 5.1.0). The licence text is in `LICENSE.ubuntu.txt`.
